Story Solitaire

Put mixed-up sentences back in order and rebuild four short stories. A free online solitaire for English learners, with a class mode.

Play Story Solitaire

Reading · sentence order · punctuation

How to play

  1. Four short stories are mixed up in the cards. Each story has its own color and emoji.
  2. Build each story pile from the first sentence to the last one.
  3. Capital letters, commas and periods help you see which card comes next.

For teachers

Class mode: the teacher chooses four stories and shares a four-letter code. Everyone plays the same deal and the class follows a live ranking.
