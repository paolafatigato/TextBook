Free online English games

Free online games for learning English: a story solitaire, a grammar mistake shooter and a card game inspired by Animal Farm. No download needed.

  • Story Solitaire

    Put mixed-up sentences back in order and rebuild four short stories. A free online solitaire for English learners, with a class mode.

  • Error Breaker

    Sentences are falling and each one has a mistake. Shoot the wrong word, then fix it. A free online error correction game for English learners.

  • Animal Farm Cards

    Grow grain, buy friends and take the farm. A free online card game in English, inspired by George Orwell’s Animal Farm, played against the computer.
