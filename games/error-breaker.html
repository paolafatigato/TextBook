Error Breaker

Sentences are falling and each one has a mistake. Shoot the wrong word, then fix it. A free online error correction game for English learners.

Play Error Breaker

Grammar · vocabulary · error correction

How to play

  1. Find the mistake in the falling sentence and shoot the wrong word.
  2. Fix it: choose the right answer or type it.
  3. Get five correct answers in a row, then hit the power stone to get more balls.

For teachers

The sentences contain typical mistakes made by Italian learners, so the game works well for revision.
